About

I'm an Honors Biomedical Engineering senior at the University of Texas at Arlington, with minors in Mathematics and Physics. My research journey has taken me across experimental biology, biomedical data analysis, machine learning, mathematical modeling, neuroimaging, and biopharmaceutical research.

I currently work with Prof. Xi Zhu in the BRAIN Lab at UTA, where I study plasma proteomics and neuroimaging in Alzheimer's disease. I am also part of UTA's NSF Research Training Group (RTG) in Mathematics for Human Health, where I work on mechanistic modeling of multidrug antibiotic resistance.

I’m motivated by curiosity, interdisciplinary problem-solving, and the chance to turn complex questions into useful knowledge. I enjoy learning new methods when a problem demands them, working across disciplines, and helping others grow along the way. Long term, I hope to build a research career developing quantitative and computational approaches that deepen our understanding of biology and disease while contributing to a collaborative and supportive scientific community.
